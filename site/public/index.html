exit arc

Arc stops working on 22 December 2026. Arc's sunset dates

exit-arc moves your Arc setup into Firefox: your spaces with their pinned tabs and folders, your Favourites, vertical tabs, a clean new tab page and your extensions. Every step is opt-in.

  • Runs locally on your Mac
  • Reads Arc's files without changing them
  • Sends nothing anywhere

What comes with you

  • Spaces, pinned tabs and folders

    One Bookmarks Toolbar folder per space, same order and nesting.

    --import

  • Favourites

    Real Firefox pinned tabs. On Zen they become Essentials.

    --pin-favourites

  • Vertical tabs

    Firefox's tab strip moves to the side.

    --vertical-tabs

  • A clean new tab

    No sponsored shortcuts, no widgets.

    --clean-newtab

  • Extensions

    Matched to their official Firefox add-ons.

    --extensions

  • Just the export

    The default. Saves everything as CSV, Markdown, JSON or HTML, and needs no browser.

    --format csv,md,json,html

Firefox is the default. Zen and Chrome work too: --to firefox|zen|chrome.

What stays behind

  • History
  • Passwords
  • Cookies
  • Extension data

For history and passwords, use Firefox's own importer (File > Import From Another Browser) or a password manager.
